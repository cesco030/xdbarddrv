1. PostgreSQL
-------------

Applies to the directory pg/ (the client library libpq of PostgreSQL and
the files of src/common, src/port and src/include it needs), to the files
    ile/ile_bitutils.c
    ile/pg_config.h
    ile/pg_config_os.h
    ile/pg_config_paths.h
    build/pg_config_paths.h
which are taken or adapted from PostgreSQL sources, and to the service
program LIBPQ built from them.  These files of pg/ were changed for ILE C:
    pg/src/include/mb/pg_wchar.h
    pg/src/include/port.h
    pg/src/interfaces/libpq/fe-connect.c
    pg/src/interfaces/libpq/fe-lobj.c
